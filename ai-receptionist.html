AI Receptionist

Your phone, answered. Every time.

A professional AI voice agent that picks up the calls you cannot, takes the job details, books it straight into your calendar and texts you a summary before you have put the phone down.

What it actually does

It is not a voicemail box and it is not a call menu. It holds a conversation, gets the information you need and acts on it.

Answers after hours and overflow

The calls that come in at nine on a Saturday, or while you are already on the other line, get picked up instead of going to voicemail.

Books straight into your calendar

It checks what you have open, offers the caller a time and puts the job in. No callback needed to arrange it.

Sends a summary of every call

Name, number, what they wanted and what was agreed, as a text to you, so you walk into the job already knowing the story.

Says exactly what you tell it to

Your pricing, your service area, your hours, your rules about what you will and will not quote over the phone.

Keeps your number

Your existing number stays yours. Calls forward only when you do not pick up, or outside the hours you set.

Hands off when it should

Call types you want to take yourself get passed straight through rather than handled.

How it works

Common questions

Will my callers know they are talking to AI?

It introduces itself as your business and speaks naturally, so most callers treat it as a normal answering service. We never instruct it to claim it is a person, and it will say so if a caller asks directly.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. Your number does not change. Calls forward to the receptionist only when you do not answer or outside the hours you set.

What happens if it gets something wrong?

Every call comes to you as a summary, so you can see what was said and follow up the same day. Anything it handles badly gets corrected in the script, and you can route specific call types straight to yourself instead.

Can I change what it says?

Yes, any time, at no extra charge. A price change, a new service, a week you are closed. You tell us and we update it.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set it up, connect it and manage it. On your end it is the same phone you already carry.

Can I hear it before signing up?

Yes. Hearing it handle a call is the fastest way to judge whether it is right for your business, so just ask.

Billing & policies

Billing

Setup fees are billed once at signup. Monthly services are billed automatically each month by card through our secure payment processor, Stripe. All prices are in USD.

Cancellation

Monthly services can be canceled at any time with 30 days' notice by phone or text. No long-term contracts. Service continues through the end of the paid period.

Refunds

Setup fees are refundable before work begins. Once setup work has started, setup fees are non-refundable. Monthly fees are not prorated or refunded for partial months.

Common questions

Will this annoy my customers?

It can, if it is set up badly. The sequences are written to stop as soon as someone replies, and the timing is deliberately spaced rather than daily.

What if someone texts back?

The reply comes to you. You take the conversation from there.

Does this need a new phone number?

No. It works with your existing number.

Can I turn parts of it off?

Yes. Some clients run missed-call text-back only and leave the rest alone. That is a perfectly reasonable place to start.

Does it work with the AI Receptionist?

Yes, and they cover different gaps. The receptionist answers the call, this one handles everything after it.

Common questions

How much do your services cost?

Follow-Up Text & Email and Google Review Management are $200 per month each. AI Receptionist, Web Design, Meta Ads and Automated Order Desk are priced by custom quote, since each business is set up differently. Some services include a one-time setup fee, which is always listed up front in your quote. Clients using two or more services receive a reduced monthly rate.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Monthly services can be canceled at any time with 30 days' notice by phone or text, and service continues through the end of the paid period.

How long does setup take?

Most clients are fully operational within two weeks. Timing depends mostly on how quickly we get access to the accounts we need to connect, such as your phone line, calendar or accounting software.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. The AI receptionist connects to your existing phone line, so customers call the same number they always have.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

Yes. Callers are told at the start of the call when it is being answered by AI or recorded. The agent is set up to sound natural and professional, and it is trained on your business's services, hours and booking rules.

Can I change what the AI receptionist says?

Yes. Greetings, answers, booking rules and anything else it says can be updated at any time. Just tell us what you want changed.

What happens if the AI makes a mistake?

Every system has a review step built in. You get a summary of every call, and with the Automated Order Desk, your team approves each order before it is entered. We also monitor performance and adjust the setup every month.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We handle the setup, connections and ongoing management. You approve everything before it goes live, and we send you a report every month.

What software do you work with?

We connect to the tools you already use, including your existing phone line, email, calendar and accounting apps like QuickBooks.

Do you work with businesses outside New Jersey?

Yes. We are based in New Jersey, but our systems are set up and managed remotely, so we work with businesses outside New Jersey as well.

Can I hear the AI receptionist before signing up?

Yes. Reach out and we will set up a time for you to hear the AI receptionist in action before you commit to anything.

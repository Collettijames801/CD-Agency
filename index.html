Billing and policies

Billing

Any one-time fees included in a quote are billed at signup. Monthly services are billed automatically each month by card through our secure payment processor, Stripe. All prices are in USD.

Cancellation

Monthly services can be canceled at any time with 30 days' notice by phone or text. No long-term contracts. Service continues through the end of the paid period.

Refunds

One-time fees are refundable before work begins. Once work has started, one-time fees are non-refundable. Monthly fees are not prorated or refunded for partial months.

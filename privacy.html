Privacy Policy and Text Message Terms

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

CD Automate is a small business based in Marlboro, New Jersey. We set up and manage AI phone answering, automated follow-up, order processing, advertising and review systems for other businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to talk with you and run the services you sign up for.

  • Contact details you give us by phone, text or email, such as your name, business name, phone number and email address.
  • Billing details, which are handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Service data created while our systems run for your business, such as call recordings and transcripts, text and email messages, appointment details and order information.
  • Basic website data, such as pages viewed and browser type, if we use simple site analytics.

How we use it

  • To respond to questions and schedule consultations
  • To set up, run, monitor and improve the services you pay for
  • To send service updates, monthly reports and invoices
  • To meet legal and accounting requirements

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to our services.

Calls handled by our AI receptionist

When a business uses our AI receptionist, calls to that business may be answered by an AI voice agent and may be recorded and transcribed. Recordings and summaries are shared with that business so it can follow up on the call. Callers are told at the start of the call when it is being answered by AI or recorded.

Information we handle for our clients

When our systems run for a client, we process the information of that client's own customers, such as callers, leads and people placing orders, on the client's behalf. That information belongs to the client. We use it only to provide the service, and we delete or return it when the client asks or when the service ends.

Text message terms

By giving a phone number and agreeing to receive texts from CD Automate or from a business that uses our services, you agree to receive messages about your inquiry, appointments, quotes, orders and service updates.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Mobile numbers and text opt-in consent are never sold or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers we use to run our business and our clients' systems, and only as much as each one needs. These include phone and texting providers, AI and voice platforms, email providers, calendar and accounting software, and Stripe for payments. Each provider is expected to protect the information and use it only to provide its service. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as it is needed to provide our services or meet legal and accounting requirements. Call recordings and messages handled for a client are kept for as long as that client's service needs them, then deleted.

How we protect it

We use reputable providers with secure, encrypted connections and limit access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to keep information safe.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below. If you are the customer of one of our clients, we may pass your request to that business, since the information belongs to them.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

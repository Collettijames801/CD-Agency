Automated Order Desk

Order emails read, checked and entered.

Incoming order emails matched against your product list, flagged when an item, a price or the stock does not look right, and entered into your accounting app once someone on your team approves it.

Request a quote How it works

Where orders go wrong

Built for businesses taking orders by email, where a misread line turns into the wrong pallet arriving at a customer site.

Reads the order

Pulls the items, quantities and details out of the email, however the customer happened to write it.

Matches to your product list

Each line matched against what you actually sell, rather than retyped by hand into a form.

Flags the problems

Missing items, prices that do not match, quantities that look wrong and stock that will not cover the order get raised before anything is confirmed.

Your team approves

Nothing is entered on its own. A person sees the order and the flags and approves it, which is where the mistakes get caught.

Enters it into your accounting app

Once approved, the order goes into apps like QuickBooks without being keyed in a second time.

Keeps the email trail

What came in, what was flagged and what was approved stays attached to the order.

Common questions

Does this replace the people handling my orders?

It is built to take the retyping off them and catch the misreads, not to remove the approval step. Somebody still signs off on every order.

What software does it work with?

It is built to work with accounting apps like QuickBooks. Tell us what you use and we will tell you honestly whether it fits.

What if an order comes in that it cannot read?

It flags it rather than guessing. Anything unclear goes to a person.

Do my customers have to change how they order?

No. They keep emailing the way they always have.

How do I know it is accurate before trusting it?

We run it against orders you have already received and processed, so you can compare what it produces against what actually happened.
